# Lotline product demo

Neutral synthetic narration; fictional stock and actions. Verbatim script below.

## 0s — An alert is only the beginning

A recall alert can reach a clinic in seconds. But it cannot inspect a stockroom. Someone still needs to find the right lot, resolve missing labels, and account for every affected unit. Lotline turns that unfinished work into a clear response record.

## 22s — Your own inventory

Sign in with an email account to use your own inventory. The workspace starts empty. Import a stock CSV, review the original recall notice, and approve its exact product and lot scope. Your records are saved to your account, separately from the sample demo. No patient information is needed.

## 48s — Uncertainty stays visible

For this walkthrough, we will switch to fictional sample stock. Sixty-four units match the reviewed recall. Twelve have no recorded lot. Thirty are outside this specific scope. Those twelve unknown units stay open for investigation. Missing information never becomes an all-clear.

## 69s — Review the source

The source snapshot shows exactly what was reviewed: one product, one lot, and instructions authorizing return to the supplier. Lotline can look up FDA references, but a person must verify the current notice and approve the scope. The software does not invent removal instructions.

## 90s — Verify the label

Here, a stockroom check confirms that all twelve unlabelled records belong to the affected lot. We enter the package identifier and retain a verification reference. Saving the check updates the assessment to seventy-six affected units, with zero unresolved. Every change is recorded in the activity history.

## 114s — Account for physical actions

Now the response moves from identification to accountability. For each affected stock record, we enter the quantity placed in quarantine and the supporting reference. Then we record the supplier return and its receipt. The server prevents disposition from exceeding quarantine, and quarantine from exceeding stock. Repeated submissions cannot double-count units. These are fictional actions for this demonstration; in use, staff must verify the physical handling and evidence.

## 158s — Completion is earned

Once all seventy-six affected units have disposition records, completion becomes available. A reviewer must confirm inventory coverage, notice amendments, and the final records. Completing the response locks it. This closes the local stock response; it does not terminate a regulatory recall or certify product safety.

## 182s — Keep a reviewable record

The evidence packet brings the source, reviewed scope, stock assessment, actions, and activity together. Download JSON or a reconciliation CSV, or print the response record. Checksums help verify file consistency. The same account and workflow work on a phone, so records remain accessible where the stock is.
